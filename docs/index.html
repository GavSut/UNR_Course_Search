Download full instructions ↓
01

Build and copy

Choose subjects and filters above. CSV is the default. Save your settings if you want to reuse them.

02

Open MyNEVADA Class Search

Sign in yourself and choose a term. Open the browser developer console and select the top context. In Firefox: ⌘ ⌥ K on Mac, or Ctrl Shift K on Windows/Linux.

03

Paste, run, and review

Paste the entire script and press Return. Keep the tab open. Review the downloaded summary for unfinished searches. Stop with UNRexport.stop(); download again with UNRexport.download().

Before you register

Matches are individual sections. Confirm required lecture/lab combinations, credits, prerequisites, permissions, and seats in MyNEVADA. TBA does not mean asynchronous. Broad searches can hit university limits; narrow your criteria and inspect any partial export.

The engine supports UNR’s observed MyNEVADA layout. Other universities need their own adapter. The website and Python app use the same search engine.
