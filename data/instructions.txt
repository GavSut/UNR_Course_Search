COURSE SEARCH STUDIO — UNR

1. Select subjects (or type any MyNEVADA subject codes), course-number limits,
   and optional title, instructor, status, component, day, or time filters.
   By default, all course numbers are eligible and the script uses the term
   already selected in MyNEVADA. To choose another term, turn that option off.

2. Choose CSV (default), JSON, or TSV under Export. Copy the generated script
   or save it as a .txt file. Keep the desktop app open until you paste.

3. Sign in to MyNEVADA yourself and open Class Search. Select the desired term.
   Open your browser's developer console and choose the top execution context.
   Firefox on Mac: Command–Option–K; Windows/Linux: Ctrl–Shift–K.
   Paste the entire generated script and press Return. Keep the tab open.

4. The script uses normal class-search controls, expands sections, follows
   recognized pages, and downloads your chosen file. Review its summary to
   see whether every requested subject finished. If blocked, run:
     UNRexport.download()
   Other formats: UNRexport.downloadCSV(), UNRexport.downloadJSON(),
   or UNRexport.downloadTSV(). Stop and save partial data: UNRexport.stop().

WHAT THE FILTERS MEAN
• All listed meetings of an individual section must fit the selected days
  and hours. Endpoints are inclusive. Hours are campus-local, 24-hour time.
• TBA, missing, overnight, or unrecognized schedules need review when the
  time/day filter is on. TBA does not prove online or asynchronous delivery.
• When time filtering is off, TBA sections can match your other criteria;
  Schedule verified will still say No.
• Comma-separated title keywords use ANY-match, ignoring case. Instructor
  accepts part of a name. Blank fields mean no restriction.
• Course numbers are numeric ranges, not a guarantee of degree applicability.
• Component filters (LEC, LAB, etc.) select individual sections. They do not
  verify linked lecture/lab enrollment combinations.
• Most filters are applied after collection. Closed sections are collected
  too, so the JSON retains the other course components for inspection.

READING THE FILE
CSV / TSV: the first data row is Search summary, followed by one row per
meeting. Match distinguishes Matches selected filters from Needs review.
The optional needs-review checkbox affects CSV/TSV only. A header plus a
summary with zero matches is a valid result. PARTIAL filenames and the
Search completeness column identify unfinished searches. Search notes name
unfinished subjects and any error. Start/end times use 24-hour time and
meeting dates use YYYY-MM-DD when recognizable.
JSON: results.matched, results.needsReview, results.excluded, and
results.unparsedRows contain the audit. courses retains raw text and rows.
The summary and searches fields describe collection progress.

LIMITATIONS AND PRIVACY
This independent tool supports UNR's observed MyNEVADA layout; it is not an
official university service or a universal adapter for other universities.
Verify required lecture/lab/discussion combinations, credits, prerequisites,
permissions, and seats in MyNEVADA. Broad searches can hit university limits;
narrow subjects or minimum course numbers and inspect any partial export.
No section-detail-only credits, delivery modes, or prerequisites are inferred.
Page layout changes may require updating the engine.
Neither builder asks for passwords or uploads your results. The generated
script works in your browser session using normal search controls and does
not enroll you. Saved settings contain filters only. GitHub Pages serves the
builder; it does not access MyNEVADA on your behalf.
